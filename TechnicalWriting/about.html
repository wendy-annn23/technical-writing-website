A little about me

Hello! I’m Wendy-Ann Najito Chitongco, a third-year BSIS student at City College of Calapan. I created this website as a learning space for our Technical Writing lessons.

I am interested in Technical Writing because it helps me express ideas clearly and organize information in a way that is easy to understand. As a BSIS student, I believe this skill will help me create better documentation, reports, and user guides in the future. I also want to improve my writing skills and share what I learn with others through this website.
