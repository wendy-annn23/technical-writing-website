TECHNICAL WRITING • LECTURE 01

Technical Writing: Definition and Purpose

Learn how technical writing communicates information clearly, accurately, and effectively.

01 • INTRODUCTION

What Is Technical Writing?

Writing notes and preparing written documents

Technical writing is a form of writing that communicates specialized, scientific, or technical information in a clear, accurate, and organized way. It helps readers understand a subject, follow instructions, or make informed decisions.

Definitions

Technical writing is characterized by formal elements such as scientific and technical vocabulary, graphic aids, and conventional report formats.

Common Features

It uses technical vocabulary, tables, graphs, figures, and organized information to make complex topics easier to understand.

04 • WHY WE WRITE

Purposes of Technical Writing

Technical writing has different purposes depending on the information being communicated and the needs of its readers.

To Inform

Provides facts, explanations, instructions, and important information to readers.

Example: A computer user manual explaining how to operate a device.

To Persuade and Influence

Uses facts, evidence, and logical arguments to encourage readers to consider a proposal or recommendation.

Example: A project proposal requesting approval for a new system.

To Analyze Events

Examines information, identifies patterns, and explains results based on evidence.

Example: A report analyzing the results of a system evaluation.

Remember this ✿
Effective technical writing is accurate, concise, objective, and organized. Its goal is to help readers understand information and use it correctly.
